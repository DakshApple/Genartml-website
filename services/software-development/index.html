05Questions

Questions about Software Development.

Ask us directly
What if my requirements change halfway?

Anything outside the signed scope is quoted as a fixed-price change request and starts only after your written approval. The original price and date stay as they were.

Which technology do you use?

We pick a widely used, well-supported stack so any good developer can maintain it after handover. The specification names it before you approve.

Can you build Android and iOS apps?

Yes. Enterprise builds include web, Android and iOS. An existing web app can also be turned into Android and iOS apps as a fixed-price add-on in 15 working days.

How do you handle downtime and security?

Standard and Enterprise builds deploy on your own cloud account. Enterprise includes a security review and load testing. After the free support period, a Care plan covers monitoring and fixes.

Do I get the source code?

Yes. The code repository, designs and documentation transfer to you on final payment.
