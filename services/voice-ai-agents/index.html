05Questions

Questions about Voice AI Agents.

Ask us directly
Which languages can the agent speak?

English, Hindi and Hinglish on every package, including callers who switch mid-sentence. Other regional languages are available on Enterprise on request, and we test them with your real callers before committing.

Will callers know they are talking to an AI?

Yes, when they ask, the agent says so plainly in one line and carries on. Hiding it is the fastest way to lose a customer's trust.

What happens when the agent does not know an answer?

It never makes one up. It says the team will confirm, logs the question and, on Growth and Enterprise, transfers the call to a person or books a callback.

What if the calling platform goes down?

During the build we agree a number that calls forward to if the platform cannot answer, so callers reach your team instead of silence. With an agent Care plan we monitor and fix it within the plan's response time.

Can it make outbound promotional calls?

Yes, within TRAI rules for commercial calls. Your scope names who owns each registration and which number is used.
