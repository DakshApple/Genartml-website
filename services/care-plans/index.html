05Questions

Questions about Care Plans.

Ask us directly
Can I get a Care plan for something Genartml did not build?

Yes, after a short audit of what is running. If something needs fixing first, we quote that at a fixed price before the plan starts.

How fast do you respond?

First response within 48 hours on Care, 24 hours on Growth and 4 working hours on Dedicated.

What counts as a small change?

Something that takes a few hours, such as a new field on a form, a copy update or a new question for a voice agent. Larger work is quoted at a fixed price.

How do I stop a plan?

Plans have a 3-month minimum and then need 30 days notice. Everything we built and maintain stays yours.

Is there a plan just for voice agents?

Yes. Agent care plans come in three levels, each at a fixed monthly fee, covering monitoring, answer updates and call quality checks.
