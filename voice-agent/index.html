11Questions

Questions, answered.

Request a free pilot
Which languages does it speak?

Hindi, English and Hinglish, including callers who switch mid-sentence.

Will callers know it is an AI?

Yes, when they ask, it says so plainly in one line and carries on.

What if it does not know an answer?

It never makes one up. It says the team will confirm, logs the question and transfers the call or books a callback.

Does it work with my existing number?

Yes. We set up call forwarding so missed or after-hours calls reach the agent.

Can it transfer to my team?

Yes, during your working hours.

How long does setup take?

Written in your proposal with a fixed date.

What do I need to provide?

Your common questions, your calendar and a phone number.

What if the calling platform goes down?

During the build we agree a number that calls forward to if the platform cannot answer, so callers reach your team instead of silence.

Can it make outbound calls?

Yes, within TRAI rules for commercial calls. Your scope names who owns each registration and which number is used. The free pilot covers inbound calls and enquiry callbacks only.
